Core Philosophy: Protect Families Without Compromise #

Elderly parents and first-time smartphone users across India lose savings daily to deceptive SMS messages: fake electricity power cut deadlines, blocked SBI/HDFC accounts, and malicious APK attachments claiming free government subsidies.

EXPERIMENTAL PROTOTYPE NOTICE

The Core Tenet: A scam protection engine must fail loudly, never optimistically. If an API quota is exhausted or network connectivity drops, SunoScam never tells a user a suspicious link is safe. It immediately cascades across 5 Gemini models and runs local deterministic heuristics.

Three Golden Protection Rules:

  • Zero-cloud dependency: If external APIs fail, deterministic heuristic evaluation flags known fraud vectors offline with zero data leakage.
  • Direct family guidance: Every alert produces an empathetic, Hindi/English response ready to copy and send to family WhatsApp groups to stop parents from clicking.
  • Sandboxed execution: Malicious APKs, EXEs, and shell files are analyzed statically by file size, headers, and MIME signatures without client execution.

Limitations & Privacy Notes #

In alignment with our principle of failing loudly:

  • Zero Message Storage: Messages, URLs, and uploaded screenshots are analyzed ephemeral in-memory. No user data or chat logs are ever stored on remote databases.
  • Autonomous Offline Fallback: If your internet drops or Gemini reaches daily quota limits, local heuristics automatically take over.
  • Always Verify with Official Branches: Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, or net banking passwords over phone calls or SMS regardless of caller ID.
